About

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at INESC-ID building privacy-preserving speech AI — from voice anonymization and cryptographic protocols to membership inference and machine unlearning — alongside broader work on speech recognition, speaker diarization, and pathological speech detection. I hold a PhD from Instituto Superior Técnico, University of Lisbon (2024), spent time as a visiting researcher at Carnegie Mellon University, and now lead the LeaF project as Principal Investigator, studying how to make speech foundation models forget.

Research focus

Privacy-preserving ML & speaker anonymization

Protecting speaker privacy in speech data and systems through voice anonymization methods, information disentanglement, and cryptographic protocols such as Fully Homomorphic Encryption and Secure Multi-Party Computation.

Machine unlearning & membership inference

Developing methods to make speech foundation models forget specific training data on request, and auditing model vulnerability to membership inference attacks.

Pathological speech detection

Detecting and monitoring conditions such as Parkinson's disease and cognitive impairment directly from speech, contributing to earlier, non-invasive diagnosis.

Speech recognition for European Portuguese

Building state-of-the-art ASR benchmarks and open speech resources for European Portuguese, including the CAMÕES benchmark and the 5,000-hour FalAR corpus.
