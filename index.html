Postdoctoral researcher working on deep learning systems for speech processing. My main expertise spans privacy-preserving machine learning — including voice conversion and anonymization, membership inference, and machine unlearning — as well as speech recognition, speaker recognition, and speaker diarization. I recently co-developed CAMÕES, an evaluation benchmark and set of state-of-the-art open-source ASR models for European Portuguese, and led the collection of FalAR, a 5,000-hour speaker-annotated speech corpus (available on Hugging Face). I also recently participated in the development of SVeritas, a robustness benchmark for automatic speaker verification. I am currently the Principal Investigator of the LeaF project (CMU Portugal), focused on machine unlearning in speech foundation models. I hold a PhD from INESC-ID / Instituto Superior Técnico, University of Lisbon (2024), with a visiting research period at the Language Technologies Institute, Carnegie Mellon University.
